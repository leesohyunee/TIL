# I can ask for a canvas and move the UI myself

_2026-10-15 · learned while polishing seashelly_

## What I learned
Describing "move this a bit left, make that smaller" in words is slow. Instead, I asked Claude to **open the page on a canvas** where I could adjust the components myself (position, size, spacing) and then apply what I set.

## Why it matters
Some design decisions are faster to make with my eyes and hands than with words. I don't have to choose between "AI does it" and "I do it". I can switch.
