# My first time hitting the usage limit

_2026-10-14 · learned while making a motion graphic for Bluebird Project_

## What I learned
On the Claude **Pro** plan, I hit my usage limit for the first time. The heaviest work was generating a **motion graphic** for my Bluebird Project page. Visual and animation work used up much more than writing or small code changes.

I also read about a new Claude tool for motion work, but at the time it was a beta for **Team plans and above**, so I couldn't try it yet.

## Why it matters
Not every task costs the same. Now I plan heavy visual work for when I have room in my limit, and keep quick edits small and specific.
