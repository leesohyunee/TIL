# I can leave comments right on a Vercel preview page

_2026-10-17 · learned while reviewing the My Outdoor Wedding page on its preview site_

## What I learned
- On the Vercel preview site for a branch, I could leave comments directly on the page, on the exact spot I wanted to change.
- I left 4 comments on the wedding page. Claude Code read them and applied all 4: removing the hero video caption, rewording one line, swapping an AI-generated photo for a real one, and moving the otter and poodle lower on the cover (it showed me 3 crop options and I picked one).
- Only after that did I say "publish" and merge into main.

## Why it matters
I don't have to describe "the caption under the second video" in chat. I point at the page itself, like reviewing a design, and the agent turns the comments into edits.
